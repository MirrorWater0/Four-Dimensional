# External Asset Sources

Three 2K PBR texture sets are included from Poly Haven and packed into `EuropeanTown.blend`:

- [Old Stone Wall 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/old_stone_wall_02)
- [Castle Brick 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/castle_brick_01)
- [Mossy Stone Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_stone_wall)

Poly Haven assets are released under CC0 1.0. The source image maps are retained in `textures/`; attribution is not required.
